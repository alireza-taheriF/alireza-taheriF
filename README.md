## 👋 About Me

I'm an AI engineer pursuing a **Master's in Artificial Intelligence**, coming from a background in **psychology**.
That mix shapes how I build: I care less about models that look clever and more about systems that **respond meaningfully to real people and real problems**.

- 🧠 **Psychology → AI** — emotion, stress, and human behaviour are recurring themes in my work
- 🔭 Working on **Persian document OCR**, **industrial AI copilots**, and **recommender systems**
- 📚 Running **100 Days of Deep Learning Papers** — reading and re-implementing one paper at a time
- 🤝 Open to collaborating on applied ML, vision-based AI, and human-centered products
